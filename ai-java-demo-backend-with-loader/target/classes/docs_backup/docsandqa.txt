
- Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid leave per year. 
Leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance 
through the HR portal. 
Unused leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.

– Expense Reimbursement Guidelines
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days using the 
Expense Management System. 
Receipts are mandatory for claims above ₹500. 
Travel reimbursements require manager approval prior to booking.


– IT Support SOP
For password resets, employees should use the self-service 
password reset tool. 
For hardware issues, raise a ticket via the IT Helpdesk portal. 
Average SLA is 24 hours for minor issues and 72 hours for 
hardware replacement.


– Project X Overview
Project X is an internal initiative to modernize the supply 
chain using AI-driven demand forecasting. 
The project is expected to reduce operational costs by 12%. 
Phase 1 focuses on inventory optimization and vendor management.


– Work From Home Guidelines
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with prior 
manager approval. 
Employees must remain online during core hours (10 AM – 5 PM IST). 
Company laptops and VPN usage are mandatory while working remotely.
